Product, release & commercial roadmap

From technical preview to the founder operating system.

A staged plan for turning TREVV’s strong portfolio, attention, decision, and execution experience into a trusted production SaaS that reduces founder coordination work with minimal input.

Assessment date 29 August 2026 Current state Hosted technical preview Earliest credible GA Q3 2027
Release truth

The interface is deployed and CI is strong, but the system is not yet a production multi-tenant SaaS. Several polished workflows still rely on deterministic seed data, optimistic local state, or browser persistence.

Planning assumption

Dates assume two strong full-stack engineers plus fractional product/design, QA, security, and operations support. A solo developer should plan roughly 12–18 months.

01 · Current assessment

A compelling product thesis, not yet a safe product promise.

Evidence below comes from the repository and official product documentation. Recommendations are proposed release decisions, not claims about what is already complete.

Verified evidence Recommendation Acceptance gate Release risk
Evidence

Strong differentiated core

Portfolio roll-ups, Attention, Waiting, Change Radar, Decisions, reviews, Insights, Blueprints, cross-workspace pressure, and stakeholder-safe views already form a coherent founder operating loop.

Evidence

Serious engineering foundation

Typed API contracts, centralized permissions, PostgreSQL/ Drizzle schema, optimistic versioning, outbox boundaries, and CI coverage create a credible path to production.

Evidence

Web-first by design

The Web/PWA is the complete demonstration surface. Expo and Tauri are API-connected foundations, not feature-parity clients—and do not need to block a Web/PWA launch.

Blocker

Persistence is incomplete

Several UI mutations—including newer Teams and Messages behavior—must move from seed or browser state into tenant-scoped typed repositories with conflict handling.

Blocker

Production services are foundations

The worker lease loop, dead letters, observability, file scanning, signed delivery, push, provider webhooks, and real-volume search still need operational implementation.

Blocker

Commercial plumbing is not live

Plan, subscription, entitlement, usage, and billing-event tables exist, but the API still returns unrestricted development entitlements and no checkout/provider lifecycle is active.

Recommended category: TREVV should be the supervisory operating layer across a founder’s work—not another generic task manager, document editor, chat replacement, or heavyweight EOS implementation. Its promise is: “Know what needs you, why it matters, and what to do next—in 30 seconds.”

02 · Market focus

Start with founders whose complexity has outgrown memory.

The strongest initial fit is a founder-led team of 2–20 people managing at least two products, clients, ventures, departments, or major workstreams.

PRIMARY 01

Founder / CEO

“Show me what is slipping, blocked, waiting, or undecided without another status meeting.”

Best fit: 5–25 person startup with multiple active workstreams.

PRIMARY 02

Portfolio founder

“Compare my ventures or clients and tell me where my intervention has the highest leverage.”

Best fit: agency, studio, venture builder, or multi-company operator.

PRIMARY 03

Chief of Staff / COO

“Turn discussions into decisions and commitments, then keep follow-up moving without manual chasing.”

Best fit: founder’s operational partner running the cadence.

SECONDARY 04

Solo founder

“Tell me the three things that deserve today and help me capture everything else without organizing it.”

Acquisition wedge and future team expansion, but lower ARPU.

SECONDARY 05

Functional lead

“Translate company priorities into team work, expose blockers, and make ownership unambiguous.”

Adoption and data-quality user; should inherit team capabilities.

SECONDARY 06

Advisor / investor / client

“Give me a concise, evidence-backed update without broad access or another meeting.”

Free viewer/guest role and a natural product-led growth loop.

Core jobs to be done

  • Know what needs the founder within 30 seconds.
  • Convert messy input into structured commitments.
  • Never lose a decision, dependency, or promised follow-up.
  • Understand why a workspace’s health changed.
  • Run a weekly review without compiling status manually.
  • Turn meetings and messages into accountable work.
  • Share progress safely with stakeholders.
  • Reuse startup operating patterns without configuration work.

03 · Minimal-input operating model

Automate coordination, preserve human judgment.

Deterministic rules should own dates, permissions, evidence, and thresholds. AI should interpret, summarize, classify, and draft. Every consequential action remains explainable and reversible.

CaptureEmail, note, voice, meeting, message, integration
ClassifyTask, decision, request, idea, waiting item
ConnectWorkspace, team, person, outcome, evidence
ConfirmOne-tap owner, due date, priority, next action
MonitorChanges, stale work, blockers, dependencies
NudgeDraft follow-up, escalate, or reschedule
ReviewOutcome, learning, consequence, next cycle

Safety ladder

Level 0 · ObserveRead and explain evidence only.
Level 1 · SuggestPropose structured work or a response.
Level 2 · InternalApply reversible TREVV changes with undo.
Level 3 · ApprovalDraft an external action; require confirmation.
Level 4 · PolicyRun narrowly scoped external actions only under explicit rules.
Never autonomous: spending money, deleting data, publishing, changing permissions, sending sensitive messages, or making consequential personnel, financial, or legal decisions.

Founder Brief

Ranks three to seven interventions with evidence, impact, and next action.

Waiting Agent

Finds external dependencies, drafts nudges, and escalates overdue commitments.

Decision Debt

Surfaces unresolved decisions blocking the most execution and preserves outcomes.

Meeting-to-Execution

Extracts decisions, tasks, owners, dates, and unanswered questions for approval.

Weekly Review

Builds an operating review from changes, risks, commitments, and completed work.

Startup Memory

Connects assumptions, evidence, decisions, consequences, and stakeholder updates.

Delegation Contract

Defines outcome, decision rights, owner, constraints, and check-in cadence.

Investor Update Builder

Drafts an evidence-backed update without exposing unrelated workspace details.

Crisis Mode

Collapses the system to critical decisions, blockers, owners, and communication cadence.

04 · Release roadmap

Reliability before reach; operating value before breadth.

Each stage is an evidence gate. Dates move if the gate is not met; the next stage does not compensate for unfinished foundations.

Stage 0

Pre-alpha foundation

Sep–Oct 2026 · 6–8 weeks

Make the hosted preview safe enough to hold real design-partner data.

Build and implementation method

  • Inventory every mutation as persisted, optimistic, local-only, seeded, or missing.
  • Persist Teams, memberships/capabilities, Messages, Work, Inbox, Decisions, Waiting, reviews, and settings through typed repositories.
  • Complete organization, portfolio, workspace, invitation, email verification, password recovery, session revocation, and membership administration.
  • Enforce tenant scope and role/action/resource policy at the API; add IDOR tests for every protected resource.
  • Separate demo and production startup paths; fail production startup when auth, database, encryption, storage, or origin configuration is absent.
  • Connect worker leasing, retry/backoff, dead letters, scheduling, and telemetry.
  • Deploy production-like staging, managed PostgreSQL, PITR, backup/restore, request tracing, error tracking, uptime checks, and alerts.
  • Update product docs so the canonical IA, Teams, and Messages match what ships.

Exit gate

  • Zero known P0/P1 security or data-integrity defects.
  • Core journey survives refresh, sign-out/in, second browser, and concurrent edit.
  • Tenant-isolation suite passes.
  • Clean migrate/seed and rollback validated.
  • Backup restoration succeeds.
  • Every CI gate passes from a clean clone.
Stage 1

Alpha

Nov–Dec 2026 · 10–20 organizations

Prove that the deterministic founder operating loop creates weekly value.

Build and implementation method

  • Ship the persistent Portfolio, Workspace, Teams, Board, My Work, Inbox, Attention, Waiting, Decisions, Reviews, and stakeholder journey.
  • Add Founder Brief, Quick Capture, and Weekly Founder Review with evidence-first deterministic logic.
  • Provide CSV import and one polished source preset with dry run and error handling.
  • Add product analytics, in-product feedback, feature flags, and an operator kill switch.
  • Manually onboard every organization and review usage weekly.
  • Publish privacy/terms, consent, export, and deletion-request processes.
  • Keep integrations mocked or disabled unless production-complete; keep mobile/desktop clearly labeled preview.
  • Use an internal free alpha-unrestricted entitlement; do not introduce payment friction yet.

Exit gate

  • 10 organizations active for four consecutive weeks.
  • ≥60% complete a weekly review in three of four weeks.
  • ≥70% of surfaced attention items judged useful or acted on.
  • Median time to first value under 20 minutes.
  • No cross-tenant exposure, unrecoverable loss, or unresolved P0/P1.
  • At least five strong “would be disappointed” customers.
Stage 2

Closed beta

Jan–Mar 2027 · 50–150 organizations

Connect real context, automate safely, and validate willingness to pay.

Build and implementation method

  • Launch self-service signup, guided onboarding, organization creation, and invitations.
  • Implement billing sandbox, invite-only live checkout, portal, webhooks, entitlements, usage, grace, cancellation, and dunning.
  • Ship automation templates with history, retry, undo, budgets, quotas, and audit trail.
  • Add guarded AI classification, summarization, follow-up drafts, and meeting extraction; measure acceptance and reversal.
  • Integrate in order: Google Calendar, GitHub and Slack, Drive metadata, then Gmail only after complete OAuth/token/webhook/MIME hardening.
  • Replace demo search with PostgreSQL full-text/trigram ranking and result snippets.
  • Add reliable collaborative invalidation, notification preferences, digest delivery, import mapping, and integration health screens.
  • Load test at 100 workspaces and 10,000 items.

Exit gate

  • 50 activated organizations.
  • Four-week organization retention ≥40%.
  • ≥60% invite at least one teammate.
  • Automation suggestion acceptance ≥60%.
  • AI reversal rate <5%; external actions always approved.
  • Integration sync success ≥99%.
  • Restore, incident, payment, and dunning drills pass.
Stage 3

Public beta

Apr–Jun 2027 · 150–200 organizations

Turn the validated operating loop into a self-service commercial product.

Build and implementation method

  • Open public signup and no-card trial; activate Founder and Startup plans.
  • Complete pricing, usage, invoice, cancellation, downgrade, and export UX.
  • Ship stage-based Blueprints for discovery, launch, fundraising, hiring, leadership review, incident, and client delivery.
  • Offer migration concierge and polished ClickUp, Asana, monday, and generic CSV import.
  • Publish help center, onboarding academy, changelog, status page, security page, subprocessors, and DPA.
  • Add integration recovery flows, production Core Web Vitals, database query monitoring, representative mobile-browser QA, and accessibility coverage.
  • Establish support triage, ownership, response targets, and customer communication.

Exit gate

  • 150–200 activated organizations.
  • Eight-week retention ≥35%.
  • Trial-to-paid target 8–15%.
  • ≥50% act on a weekly Founder Brief.
  • ≥30% complete the weekly review.
  • Availability ≥99.7% over eight weeks.
  • At least 10 referenceable paying customers.
Stage 4

General availability

Q3 2027 · Web/PWA production

Make a durable, supportable production promise—without waiting for native parity.

Build and implementation method

  • Complete independent security review and penetration test; close critical/high findings.
  • Operate a 99.9% availability SLO with error budgets, on-call ownership, incident communication, and public status.
  • Demonstrate RPO ≤15 minutes and RTO ≤4 hours through a restore exercise.
  • Verify data export/deletion, retention controls, audit evidence, and provider revocation.
  • Exercise billing renewal, downgrade, cancellation, refund, failed payment, invoice, and tax paths.
  • Require provider health, reconnect, signature verification, rate limiting, and deletion behavior for every production integration.
  • Pass representative performance at 100+ workspaces and 10,000+ work items.
  • Audit product and marketing claims against actual behavior.

Launch gate

  • All security, privacy, restore, SLO, billing, and scale controls pass.
  • No production-critical demo path remains.
  • Stable paid-cohort retention and five detailed customer references.
  • Support and escalation commitments are staffed.
  • Native clients are clearly “companion preview” if not parity-ready.
Stage 5

Post-GA

Evidence-led, not calendar-led

Expand only where retained customers demonstrate repeated need.

Candidate work

  • Full email-provider breadth and deeper provider reconciliation.
  • Mobile My Work, Inbox, Quick Capture, approvals, push, and offline mutation queue.
  • Signed desktop apps, tray/global capture, secure vault, and update pipeline.
  • Enterprise SSO/SCIM, SIEM/DLP, domain control, and data residency options.
  • Advanced timeline/Gantt or time tracking only with strong demand evidence.

Prioritization rule

  • Repeated request from retained, paying ICP customers.
  • Clear effect on activation, retention, expansion, or trust.
  • Operational cost and security burden understood.
  • Does not weaken the 30-second founder promise.

06 · Learning and growth system

Measure resolved founder attention, not activity volume.

The product succeeds when founders spend less time coordinating and more time resolving the few high-leverage issues that require judgment.

North star

Qualified attention actions resolved

Per active organization, per week.

<20 min

Time to first value

From signup to first useful brief or resolved attention item.

≥40%

Closed-beta W4 retention

Measured at the organization level, not individual logins.

<5%

AI reversal rate

For accepted structured suggestions and automations.

Activation

  • Create or import two workspaces.
  • Invite one teammate.
  • Resolve one Attention item.
  • Record one Decision.
  • Complete one review within seven days.

Value and trust

  • Founder Brief action rate.
  • Waiting and decision latency reduction.
  • Automation acceptance/edit/reversal.
  • Self-reported admin time saved.
  • Sync, permission, restore, and data-loss reliability.

Commercial health

  • Trial-to-paid and plan mix.
  • W4/W8 organization retention.
  • Expansion and operator-pack utilization.
  • AI gross margin per resolved action.
  • Support burden per active organization.

Distribution sequence

  1. High-touch design partners from founder communities, venture studios, agencies, accelerators, and fractional COO networks.
  2. Free Founder Operating Health assessment that creates a useful starter workspace.
  3. Migration concierge from spreadsheets and incumbent work tools.
  4. Shareable stakeholder updates with subtle TREVV attribution.
  1. Stage-specific Blueprint library as SEO, education, and activation content.
  2. Partner program for chiefs of staff, startup coaches, VCs, and accelerators.
  3. GitHub, Slack, and calendar marketplace distribution after integrations are stable.
  4. Annual anonymized Founder Operations Benchmark only with explicit consent and privacy review.

07 · Risks and required decisions

Protect focus, trust, and runway.

The largest threat is not a missing feature. It is presenting a broad, polished demonstration as dependable automation before persistence, operations, and trust controls are ready.

Risk Mitigation and release decision
Local/demo state looks production-ready Complete the mutation inventory and persistence gate before alpha; label or disable every preview-only path.
Product becomes a generic everything app Every roadmap item must strengthen the 30-second brief, decision quality, waiting follow-up, or operating cadence.
AI destroys trust Evidence links, confidence, approval, audit, undo, budgets, and deterministic fallback are mandatory.
Integration scope delays launch Sequence Calendar → GitHub/Slack → Drive → Gmail; do not promise broad provider support prematurely.
Cheap pricing creates unsustainable support/AI cost Bundle operators, meter expensive actions, cap usage by default, and test willingness to pay before GA.
Native parity consumes the roadmap Launch Web/PWA independently; expand native clients only after retained customers demonstrate need.
Founder Brief produces noise Measure usefulness, action, dismissal, and false positives; tune evidence thresholds before adding more signals.
1

Decide the primary wedge during alpha.

Is retained value driven by multi-workspace portfolio visibility, the Founder Brief, or the weekly operating review?

2

Decide whether TREVV supervises or replaces work tools.

The recommended path is supervisory: ingest signals, preserve decisions, and coordinate attention without recreating every specialist tool.

3

Decide the two integrations that create 80% of value.

Validate with design partners before committing to broad email, chat, drive, CRM, and developer-platform scope.

4

Decide what “operator” means commercially.

Use an explicit billing role—not invisible rolling activity—to keep pricing predictable and collaboration-friendly.

Explicitly defer: full Slack replacement, full CRM, accounting, invoicing, payroll, advanced Gantt, broad autonomous agents, and native feature parity. Build them only when retained, paying customers show repeated need.
